## Services (the REAL list — 10, vs the 6 currently on /services)

**Airline products and services:**
1. **Airlines Reservations and Ticketing** — comparing multiple airlines,
   crafting efficient itineraries, best value within travel policies and
   safety standards.
2. **Visa & Travel Documentation Support** — all visa processes, temporary or
   permanent; country-specific guidance, documentation and applications; visa
   renewals and extensions at competitive rates.
3. **Hotel & Accommodation Arrangements** — sourced and compared globally;
   hotels, apartments, shared spaces, bed & breakfast; short- and long-term
   stays.
4. **Safety & Protocol Management Services** — tailored protocol and local
   security arrangements for business or leisure journeys.
5. **Airport Transfers & Ground Transportation** — vetted, reliable transport
   network; executives delivered safely to hotels, meetings, destinations.
6. **Flight Charter Services (Private Jet & Helicopter)** — short- and
   long-haul charter for executive meetings, private vacations, exclusive
   destination events.
7. **Group Travel Planning & Packages** — tailored group tours, corporate
   retreats, incentive travel programs; itinerary planning, logistics
   coordination, destination management.

**Other travel services:**
8. **Travel Insurance Solutions** — medical emergencies, trip cancellations,
   baggage protection.
9. **Proof of Funds Assistance** — support for study/relocation-abroad
   financial requirements, competitive rates.
10. **Specialized Travel Experiences** — religious (pilgrimage), leisure, or
    celebratory; sightseeing adventures, destination wedding travel.
